# DishPix — See it. Snap it. Eat it.

DishPix is a simple social web application where food lovers share photos of their meals and rate their favorite restaurant dishes. It helps you explore great food by seeing how friends rate their plates, joining conversations in the comments, and liking the best dishes in your circle.

## 🚀 Core Features

* **Food Logging:** Quickly snap a photo, name the dish, and tag the restaurant.
* **Dish Ratings:** Rate individual dishes to share your honest opinion.
* **Social Feed:** Explore what your friends are eating in real time.
* **Community Interaction:** Leave comments on posts and like the best dishes in your circle.
